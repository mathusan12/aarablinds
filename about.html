About AARA

Crafted for Modern Living

Since 2010, AARA Blinds & Shutters has been helping homeowners, designers, and builders transform interiors with premium custom window coverings designed for style, privacy, and everyday comfort.

Our Story

A Tradition of Quality Since 2010

AARA Blinds & Shutters was founded in 2010 with a vision to provide elegant, functional, and high-quality window coverings for homes and commercial spaces across Ontario.

From day one, our focus has been on craftsmanship, premium materials, precise measurements, and professional service. We believe window coverings should do more than simply cover a window — they should enhance the atmosphere of a room and reflect the character of the space.

Whether it’s blinds, shutters, or custom solutions for larger projects, our team works closely with each client to ensure every detail is thoughtfully considered from consultation to installation.
